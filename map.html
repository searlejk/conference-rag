Speaker map

Where each speaker's talks fall among the subjects of General Conference, 2020–2025

Each small dot is one talk, colored by topic. Each talk's meaning (a 1,536-number vector built from its nouns, verbs, and adjectives plus its full text) is squeezed onto this 2D map with UMAP, so talks about similar things sit near each other. A speaker's diamond is placed at the average meaning of all their talks. Distances are approximate — UMAP keeps neighborhoods, not exact distances — and speakers with few talks in these years (shown in the list) have less certain positions.
